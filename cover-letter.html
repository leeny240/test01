Dear Hiring Manager,

I first used Speak because I was afraid to speak directly with a native English speaker.

I was serious about improving my English and studied alongside a full-time job, but starting a conversation still felt intimidating. Speak gave me a way to practice before taking that step. I remember being impressed by how well it recognized my speech. As a learner, that was exciting. As a designer, it made me curious about how technology could make speaking feel less daunting.

That experience is why this role interests me. Speaking practice involves more than knowing vocabulary or grammar. It also means being willing to make mistakes, receive feedback, and try again. I’d like to help design those moments so learners feel comfortable continuing, even when they struggle.

I bring over 13 years of design experience, including five and a half years working on B2B and Saas Product. As a hands-on Design Lead, I worked on UX/UI, onboarding, design systems, and product improvements with product managers and engineers. I enjoy organizing unclear requirements, making design decisions tangible, and refining the details through collaboration.

I’m now looking to focus on hands-on product design in a global team. Speak is particularly meaningful to me because I know the hesitation that can come before speaking and I’d like to help more learners take that first step.
